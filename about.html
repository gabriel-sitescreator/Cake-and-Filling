About Cake & Filling

Cake & Filling is a free visual library created for bakeries, cake shops, pastry businesses and dessert brands.

The idea is simple: make it easier for small businesses to find beautiful bakery images they can use in their everyday marketing.

Creating content for a bakery can take time. Not every business has a photographer, a designer or a large collection of product photos. Cake & Filling was created to provide another option — a growing library of AI-generated cake, dessert and bakery images that are free to download and use.

Made for bakery marketing

The images can be used for many everyday business needs, including social media posts, Pinterest, websites, digital menus, promotions, WhatsApp, newsletters and other marketing materials.

Some images focus simply on the cake or dessert itself. Others will be created specifically for marketing situations, such as promotions, menus, website banners and seasonal campaigns.

A growing library

Cake & Filling is an ongoing project.

The library will continue to grow with new cakes, desserts, styles, formats and marketing visuals. The goal is to build a useful collection that makes finding the right image quick and simple.

Free to use

The images are available to download for free. You can crop, resize and edit them for your own projects, including commercial marketing.

Because the images are AI-generated, they should be treated as visual content rather than exact representations of real products. If you are promoting a specific cake or dessert, make sure the image accurately represents what you actually sell.

Cake & Filling exists to make visual content a little easier for the people who make, sell and share great food.

Support Cake & Filling

Every image on Cake & Filling is free.

If the library is useful to you and you would like to help keep the project growing, you can optionally support it with a small contribution.

Contributions are completely optional. Downloading and using the images does not require payment or an account.
